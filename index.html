01 / Selected research

Work grounded in real systems.

My current direction connects rigorous reasoning with the messy reality of production software: proofs must scale, benchmarks must be trustworthy, and failures must be represented faithfully.

Formal methods / active Current

TLAPS-Bench: evaluating AI-generated TLA+ proofs

Contributing benchmark and evaluator infrastructure for machine-assisted formal reasoning. My work includes cache-aware and sharded TLAPM verification for large proofs, continuation and failure-recovery strategies, resume correctness, and model execution controls.

See contributions ↗
Dependable systems UIUC

Real-world failures for agentic SRE

Studying how AI agents diagnose and recover from production failures safely. I built a reproducible SREGym scenario for Kubernetes node conntrack exhaustion, including setup, observability, and oracle logic.

Speech representations

What do multilingual speech encoders learn about Bengali?

My undergraduate research used speaker-disjoint, layer-wise probing to study Bengali phone-like units across Whisper variants, with XLS-R comparison and robustness analyses. This work was accepted to INTERSPEECH 2026.
