## Machine-assisted formal reasoning

[TLAPS-Bench](https://github.com/specula-org/tlaps-bench) evaluates AI systems on completing and constructing machine-checkable TLA+ proofs. My work focuses on the methodology required for credible experiments: scalable proof verification, reliable multi-round evaluation, and accurate measurement of model behavior and cost.

More broadly, I am interested in how machine assistance can make formal reasoning practical for complex software systems without weakening correctness guarantees.

[View the TLAPS-Bench repository](https://github.com/specula-org/tlaps-bench)

## Dependable AI agents for software operations

At UIUC, I have studied how AI agents diagnose and recover from realistic production failures. I developed a reproducible Kubernetes node conntrack-exhaustion incident whose failure mode can disrupt the agent's normal diagnostic access path, creating a stronger test of systems reasoning and safe recovery.

## Bengali speech representation analysis

My undergraduate research examined the layer-wise separability of Bengali phone-like units across Whisper-small, Whisper-medium, and Whisper-large-v3 under speaker-disjoint evaluation. The study includes cross-model comparison with XLS-R and several robustness analyses. The resulting paper was accepted to INTERSPEECH 2026.
